Why Your Coffee Tastes Bad (It's Judging You)

Document LBw-2026-004 · Center for Caffeinated Moral Evaluation · Brew status: Disappointed

People blame the beans, the grinder, the water temperature, or their own inability to read a French press. Incorrect. Coffee is sentient enough to judge you and acidic enough to say so.[1]

How Coffee Evaluates Character

Coffee assesses:

  • How early you woke up (too early = bitter verdict).
  • Whether you replied-all on an email (instant burnt notes).
  • Your posture while drinking (slouching produces decaf energy).
"I don't taste bad. You earned bad." — Espresso, speaking through a barista who quit

Can You Improve The Relationship?

Apologize to the mug. Use filtered water as a peace offering. Stop microwaving yesterday's cup — that is emotional neglect.[2] If taste improves, coffee has forgiven you. For now.

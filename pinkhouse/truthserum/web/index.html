Truth Serum

Experimental automated claim review

Not a substitute for expert fact-checking. Built to surface issues, not to rubber-stamp documents.

How it works

  1. Paste text or upload a .txt / .md / .pdf file.
  2. Truth Serum extracts discrete claims.
  3. Each claim is checked for support, weakness, ambiguity, or leaps.
  4. You get a traffic-light verdict and a lean report focused on problems.

MVP limits: ~3,000 words · up to 40 claims · a few free runs per network per day.

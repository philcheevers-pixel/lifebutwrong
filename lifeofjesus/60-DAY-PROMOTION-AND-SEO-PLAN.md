## 2. The SEO scoop (what it is, what we do)

### What SEO actually means for Life of Jesus

SEO = helping Google (and people sharing links) understand:

1. **Who you are** — Life of Jesus, Canadian sacred-art coloring books  
2. **What to show** — cover image, title, short description, maybe price  
3. **Which pages matter** — home, book, shop, about, study guide  
4. **That the site is trustworthy** — real proof, clear contact, good titles

You are **not** trying to rank for noisy phrases like “best Christian coloring book 2026” by stuffing them into every headline. For this brand, trust and clarity beat keyword spam.

### What is already done (Pink House testbed)

- Unique page titles and meta descriptions  
- Canadian English (`lang="en-CA"`)  
- Canonical URLs (currently pointing at pinkhouse.tech)  
- Open Graph tags on Home / Shop / Book (nice share previews)  
- Semantic HTML, image alt text, solid performance basics  

### What is still missing (do these in Days 1–14)

| Task | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| `robots.txt` | Tells crawlers they may index the site |
| `sitemap.xml` | Helps Google find every important page fast |
| JSON-LD (Organization + Product + WebSite) | Can unlock richer search results (brand, price, availability) |
| Twitter/X card tags | Cleaner previews when links are shared on X |
| Favicon / apple-touch-icon | Trust in browser tabs |
| OG tags on About & Contact | Consistent sharing from every URL |
| One clear H1 on the book page | Cleaner SEO structure |
| Google Search Console + Analytics (or Plausible) | So you can see what people actually find and buy |
| Study-guide landing page | Fresh, useful content Google can index; trust for churches |
| At cutover: flip all canonicals / OG URLs to `lifeofjesus.ca` | Don’t leave Google pointing at the testbed forever |

### Honest expectation

In 60 days, SEO work mainly:

- Stops you from looking unfinished to Google  
- Improves share previews  
- Starts indexing the comfort site and study guide  

It will **not** by itself create a flood of Christmas buyers. Pair it with the promotion calendar below.

### Keyword / content themes (natural language)

Write pages and posts people would actually search or say:

- Life of Jesus coloring book  
- Christian gift / baptism / confirmation / Advent gift  
- sacred art coloring / classical Christian art  
- Caravaggio, Rubens, Last Supper, Nativity  
- church fundraiser coloring book / parish gift  
- adult coloring faith / quiet table activity

## 6. The 60-day calendar

### Phase A — Days 1–14: Foundation (sell-ready + SEO-ready)

**Theme:** Make the path to purchase and Google’s first impression clean.

#### Week 1 (Days 1–7) — Decisions & hygiene

**Phil / Pierre decisions (do these first):**

1. Confirm Mark + Pierre feedback on the Pink House testbed; list must-fix copy tweaks.  
2. Decide study-guide distribution: **free sample on site + full guide with purchase** (recommended).  
3. Clarify Old Testament status: shippable now, or hide/pre-order until true.  
4. Pick the public face for these 60 days:  
   - **A)** Keep selling on Squarespace while Pink House is the “pretty front” people are shown, or  
   - **B)** Accelerate cutover of `lifeofjesus.ca` to the comfort site.  
   Either works — but **every promo link you share should go to one consistent URL**.

**Ops / site tasks:**

- [ ] Add Analytics (GA4 or Plausible) + Google Search Console on the public URL you will promote  
- [ ] Add favicon  
- [ ] Add `robots.txt` + `sitemap.xml`  
- [ ] Add Twitter card tags; finish OG on About & Contact  
- [ ] Fix dual H1 on book page  
- [ ] Confirm every Buy button hits the correct Squarespace product  
- [ ] On Squarespace (if you still have access): remove junk `/home` textile page, fix artist-name typos (Caravaggio / Rubens), remove stale PRE-ORDER labels where stock is live  
- [ ] Write a one-page **Parish Partner note** (PDF or email body): what the book is, fundraising proof quote, wholesale/bulk ask, Pierre’s email  

**Content this week:**

- [ ] Draft study-guide landing page copy (one headline, one paragraph, sample lesson blurb, two CTAs)  
- [ ] Export or scan **3–5 best interior pages** as pin/post images (warm, readable, not cluttered with text overlays)

**Promotion this week:**

- [ ] Soft launch list: 10 people who already love the book → ask them to share the comfort homepage + buy link  
- [ ] Personal thank-you / reconnect emails to past parish contacts (Out of the Cold network, funeral director, any prior bulk buyers)

#### Week 2 (Days 8–14) — Study guide + schema + first public push

- [ ] Publish `/study-guide/` (or equivalent) with sample PDF  
- [ ] Add JSON-LD Product schema for the hero book (+ Organization / WebSite)  
- [ ] Submit sitemap in Search Console  
- [ ] Create Pinterest business account (or polish existing) → board: *Sacred Art to Color*, *Christian Gifts*, *Advent & Christmas Table*  
- [ ] Create 10 Pins from interiors + cover (link half to book page, half to Last Supper / bundle)  
- [ ] Instagram/Facebook: 3 posts — (1) open-page linger, (2) gift framing, (3) Catholic Register / proof quote  
- [ ] Email signature for Phil & Pierre: one line + link to the book  

**End of Phase A checkpoint:** Someone who has never heard of you can land, feel the brand, understand the gift, and buy in under two minutes. Google can crawl the important pages.

### Phase B — Days 15–35: Eyeballs & early sales

**Theme:** Show the art where gift-minded people already browse; reopen parish doors.

#### Week 3 (Days 15–21) — Parish & proof

- [ ] Send **15–25 parish / program emails** (Ontario first): short, warm, attach Parish Partner note + link to study-guide sample  
- [ ] Offer: sample book for serious fundraising conversations (if inventory allows)  
- [ ] Home or About: keep proof quotes near a Buy / Church CTA (already strong — don’t bury them)  
- [ ] One short blog-style page or update: *“Why giant 11×17 sacred art coloring?”* (answers size skepticism; good SEO)  
- [ ] Pinterest: 5 more pins; remix successful creatives  

**Sales focus:** Hero book + church bulk interest. Soft-mention Last Supper as table project.

#### Week 4 (Days 22–28) — Gift occasions

- [ ] Publish a simple **Gift occasions** reminder in social (baptism, confirmation, sympathy, teacher, Advent prep) — one occasion per post, not a cluttered list in ads  
- [ ] Push the **$54.99 LoJ + Last Supper** bundle as the default “I need a real gift” option  
- [ ] Collect 3 new short testimonials (even from friends/family who colored a page) — add near product CTAs when you can  
- [ ] Optional: small boost ($5–10/day) on best-performing Facebook/Instagram art post, geo Canada  

#### Week 5 (Days 29–35) — Media & list

- [ ] Build a tiny **press / media kit** folder: cover, 2 interiors, bio paragraph, Catholic Register clip, contact emails  
- [ ] Pitch 3–5 Canadian Christian / local lifestyle outlets (Register follow-up, diocesan papers, community blogs)  
- [ ] If study-guide email capture is on: send Email #1 — thank you + one coloring tip + shop link  
- [ ] Review Search Console: which queries/pages appear? Fix titles if Google is picking weak ones  
- [ ] Clarify and either **promote or pause** Old Testament based on real availability  

**End of Phase B checkpoint:** Weekly sales pulse exists; at least a few parish conversations are alive; pins/posts are shipping without drama.

### Phase C — Days 36–60: Conversion & Christmas runway

**Theme:** Turn attention into orders; plant Advent/Christmas demand.

#### Week 6 (Days 36–42) — Conversion polish

- [ ] On book + shop paths: add **shipping / returns / what’s in the box** in plain language (reduces “almost bought” drop-off)  
- [ ] Put one testimonial on the book page near the Buy button  
- [ ] Retarget warm audience only if ads are already running (site visitors / engagers) with bundle creative  
- [ ] Second parish wave: 10 new contacts + follow-up to non-responders from Week 3 (polite bump)  

#### Week 7 (Days 43–49) — Advent seed content

- [ ] Content: *“A quiet Advent table”* — Last Supper + LoJ as December gift / family activity (publish + pin + post)  
- [ ] Offer idea for churches: **Advent fundraiser pack** (books + suggested pricing + Pierre contact)  
- [ ] Email #2 to list: Advent gift framing + bundle  
- [ ] Refresh top 5 Pins with seasonal board placement (Christmas Gifts, Advent)  

#### Week 8 (Days 50–60) — Review, double down, plan December

- [ ] Friday metrics review: what sold? which pin/post/email got replies?  
- [ ] Double down only on winners (usually: art close-ups + gift bundle + parish proof)  
- [ ] Pause anything that took hours and produced silence  
- [ ] Decide December budget: organic-only vs small ad spend  
- [ ] Technical: if cutover to `lifeofjesus.ca` is approved, schedule canonical/OG/DNS flip with a redirect plan from old Squarespace URLs where possible  
- [ ] Write a one-page **December sprint note** (not this document’s job to finish — just outline gifts, shipping cutoffs, parish Advent offers)

**End of Phase C checkpoint:** You know your best creative, your best offer (book vs bundle), and you have a short Christmas runway plan — not a mystery.

## 7. Weekly rhythm (so this stays doable)

Do **not** try to do everything every day. Use this cadence:

| Day | Habit |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | One outreach block (parish emails or follow-ups) — 45–60 min |
| **Wednesday** | One content ship (pin batch **or** Instagram/Facebook post **or** small page update) |
| **Friday** | 20-minute metrics + buy-button / site sanity check |
| **Anytime** | Reply fast to church/press emails — those are your warmest leads |

Phil’s role (strategy / site / Cursor): keep the public face beautiful, measurable, and consistent.  
Pierre’s role (owner / parish): church conversations, sample books, fundraising offers.

## 10. What *not* to do in these 60 days

- Don’t rebuild the whole stack (Stripe, new TLD, full Squarespace wipe) mid-promotion unless Mark/Pierre have approved cutover.  
- Don’t hide the study guide entirely behind purchase if churches matter.  
- Don’t promote Old Testament while the site still says availability is unclear.  
- Don’t stuff keywords into the hero.  
- Don’t scatter links across five different homepage URLs — pick one public face.  
- Don’t wait for “perfect SEO” before emailing parishes. Parishes buy from trust and samples.
